A grassland survival

Emberfall

Late autumn on the downs. You have the clothes you stand in and perhaps four hours of usable daylight. The meadow is open and nearly bare — good stone, dry grass, a few berry canes. The wood to the east is thick with deadfall and standing timber, and it breaks the wind.

Nothing here will kill you quickly except the cold. Twist cord from fibre, cut an axe, make a bow drill, and get a fire lit before the light goes. Then build something to sleep under.
