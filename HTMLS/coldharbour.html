A survival game on the downs

Coldharbour

A coldharbour is an old field-name: a bare shelter beside the road where travellers spent the night out of the wind. There isn't one here. There is open chalk grassland, a wood along the eastern rise, and about ten hours of light.

Cut fibre and twist it into cord. Knap an axe, make a bow drill, ring a hearth with stone. Fell timber and raise a lean-to over it, and the night is survivable. Fail to, and the wind will take about four degrees an hour out of you.
